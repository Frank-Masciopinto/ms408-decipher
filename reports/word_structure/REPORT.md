# Word structure of MS 408 (Stages 6 and 7): slot grammar, affixes, behavioural clusters

> **MS 408 is undeciphered.** This stage measures *structure* (how well a positional grammar fits the vocabulary; whether frequent words fall into stable behavioural groups) and runs the identical measurements on controls. Slots are numbered positions, clusters are numbered groups; **no cluster is called a verb, noun, name or anything grammatical**, and a stable grouping is not evidence of syntax, only of distributional regularity. See [docs/WORD_STRUCTURE.md](../../docs/WORD_STRUCTURE.md).

Setup: MS 408 running text (ZL 3b, collapsed EVA units: ch, sh, cth, ckh, cph, cfh), 34977 tokens; controls are the same size-matched samples as in `reports/controls/` (Latin incl. herbal/medical, Italian, German, Hebrew/Arabic consonantal, ciphers, null generators). Slot grammar K=12. Cross-validation by block (folio for MS 408, 500-token chunk for controls), 4 folds. MS 408 CIs: delete-one-block jackknife over 8-page blocks (`ms408_ci.csv`; estimate ± 1.96 SE).

## 2. Slot grammar, prefix-core-suffix, MDL

**Slot grammar** (12 ordered slots; each slot is skipped or emits one unit from its own distribution; fitted by EM, so slots are learned, not hand-written). Held-out (4-fold by folio) cost, bits:

| model | bits/type | bits/token | bits/NEW type | shuffled-null bits/type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unit unigram | 25.23 | 21.18 | 28.09 | 24.04 |
| within-word unit bigram | 17.15 | 12.37 | 20.19 | 22.79 |
| slot grammar K=12 | 19.72 | 15.50 | 22.77 | 22.64 |
| prefix-core-suffix | 17.28 | – | 20.43 | – |
| token lexicon (+escape to bigram) | – | 11.93 | – | – |

- The slot grammar recovers 68% of the unigram→bigram information gain (type level) and 64% (token level), but is still **2.57 bits/type worse than the within-word bigram** with ~28% as many parameters (178 vs 644). Description length (data + 0.5·log2 N per parameter): slot 21.25, bigram 18.98, unigram 26.77, slot on within-word-shuffled MS 408 23.71 bits/type (the shuffled text needs 2.47 more).
- Shuffling units inside words makes the slot grammar 2.93 bits/type (4.70 bits/token) worse: the positional structure is real in the sense that it compresses.
- Coverage (pruned grammar can generate the held-out type): slot 98.3%, seen-bigram baseline 98.4%; of the 47% of held-out types NEVER seen in training, slot grammar generates 96.5%, bigram baseline 96.6%. Coverage is high for both because the pruning threshold is permissive; bits are the sharper measure.
- Prefix-core-suffix: 39 learned prefixes, 34 suffixes; 82% of types get a prefix, 21% a suffix; distinct cores per type 0.38; held-out 17.28 bits/type (+0.13 vs bigram).

## Caveats

- **Undeciphered.** Distinctive statistics do not identify a mechanism, a language or a meaning; the same numbers may arise from several processes (including the generators here, whose inventories were written with Voynichese in mind).
- The slot grammar is *one* positional model (independent emissions per slot, one unit per slot, K=12). A bigger or different model (slot-conditioned bigrams, Zattera's exact slot table, Stolfi's crust-mantle-core) could fit better; the unit bigram is only a baseline, not a ceiling.
- Words longer than K=12 units are dropped from every model (share reported as `share_over_K`; large for verbose/nomenclator-style ciphers, which therefore are not like-for-like).
- Units differ: MS 408 uses collapsed EVA units (ch, sh, cth, ckh, cph, cfh); controls use single characters (Hebrew/Arabic one native letter). Alphabet sizes differ (~22 vs 23-33), which shifts absolute bits; ratios (`slot gain frac`, null gaps) are less sensitive but not immune.
- Cross-validation folds are blocks (folios / 500-token chunks), not independent texts: MS 408 pages share hand, section, and vocabulary. Held-out numbers are optimistic for within-section generalisation and say nothing about unseen sections.
- Clusters use only the 200 most frequent word types; ARI/silhouette depend on that cut-off, k and the SVD dimension. Behavioural tags are descriptive thresholds (1.5× baseline), nothing more.
- Control rows are one sample each (stochastic controls: seed 1); family prediction intervals mix base texts and are optimistic about 'all texts of that kind'. MS 408 SEs are a delete-one-8-page-block jackknife (no quire map; sampling noise only).
- Neighbour features treat MS 408 as flat running text (line breaks ignored) for the control comparison; the `line`/`full` variants use line and metadata information controls do not have, so they are reported for MS 408 only.
