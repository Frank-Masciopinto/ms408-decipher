## 7. Faithfulness, limitations and what was not done

* **Naibbe**: tables, card weights, respacing probability and the unambiguity rule are the author's (MIT code/tables, Greshko 2025, commit f2675ec); the full text of the Cryptologia paper sits behind a bot wall and could not be fetched, so details were taken from the released code and from paper excerpts in search snippets (card-table mapping 5:2:2:2:1:1, 52/78-card decks, unigram/bigram respacing, Zattera-style slot grammar for affixes). The author's *Voynichesque* model and spreadsheets were not used. No nulls, line or paragraph structure (the paper lists this as a known limitation); one fresh deck per ~60 plaintext letters (the author's code uses one per plaintext line).
* **Self-citation**: run through the authors' own Java source (JDK 17, log4j 1.2.17), not re-implemented, because the algorithm is defined by ~5,000 lines of rules; the paper's text (behind a bot wall) was only seen in snippets. Parameters swept are those exposed in `conf.properties` inside their documented ranges; the `voynich` / `statistic` / `percent` follow rules are excluded as circular. The generator embeds VMS-derived priors (glyph classes, the f103v seed line, `daiin`/`ol`/`chedy` suggestion types), so a good match is not independent evidence.
* **Zattera**: the 12-slot table is transcribed from Zandbergen's reproduction (voynich.nu/a3_para.html §3.5.4) of Zattera (2022); the paper's own probabilities and its transliteration alphabet were not available, so the hand-coded part is the slot *structure*; probabilities are EM-fitted here. Coverage depends on the transcription used here (ZL 3b, collapsed EVA).
* **Stolfi**: the normal-word PCFG with its counts is transcribed from the 2000 note; the abnormal-word lists are not part of the grammar and are counted as uncovered. The 'published probabilities' variant was fitted by Stolfi on the whole 1998 text, so it is *not* a held-out result; the re-fitted variant re-estimates rule probabilities on the training folios only.
* **In-sample generators**: the grammar generators (learned slot, slot-conditioned bigram, Zattera, Stolfi) are fitted to all of MS 408 and sampled i.i.d. They show what a word grammar *alone* can reproduce (an upper bound), not an independent test; they have no memory between words, so repetition / page-level structure is expected to be missed.
* No metric here is decisive and many are correlated; the scorecard counts are descriptive. Verdict widths depend on the number of runs.
