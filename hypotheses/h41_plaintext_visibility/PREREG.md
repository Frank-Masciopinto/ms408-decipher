# h41: can a long-range statistic see a plaintext at MS sample size? (pre-registration)

Written before any h41 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
h27 and h34–h39 could not tell Boccaccio from Zipf nonsense on the short-range fingerprint. Before fitting another generator, test whether any long-range statistic can tell them apart at the manuscript's sample size. Apply to MS 408 only a statistic that passes that test.

## Streams
Each stream is 10,500 word tokens.

- **real:** five contiguous non-overlapping windows of Boccaccio (`it_boccaccio`), starts at token 0, 10500, 21000, 31500, 42000.
- **shuffle / zipf:** h27's builders and `encrypt_swap` (real Naibbe tables, suffix-swap copy at 5%), seeds 1..5. Regenerate the ciphertext. Do not rerun the likelihood scorer.
- **C0:** fit the h12 C0 engine once on hand 2B's paragraph words (seed 0) and generate five streams, seeds 1..5. If the fit's random search is reduced for time, record the reduced `n_random` and do not compare C0 in the separation test.
- **MS:** hands 1A, 2B, 3B, first 10,500 paragraph-line words, ZL3b with comma as a space. Score MS only on statistics that pass.

## Statistics
Words are atoms. Blocks for burstiness and block divergence are 200 tokens.

- **new_type:** fraction of types in the second half that do not occur in the first half.
- **burst:** among the 50 most frequent types, the mean of (count in the type's busiest block) / (count / number of blocks).
- **mi100:** word-identity mutual information at lag 100, divided by mutual information at lag 1. Lags with no pairs contribute nothing. If lag-1 MI is 0, the ratio is 0.
- **block_js:** mean Jensen–Shannon divergence, in bits, between the unigram distributions of adjacent blocks.

## Separation rule
A statistic passes only if both of these hold on the five real windows against the five shuffle seeds and against the five zipf seeds:

- the real values lie entirely above the shuffle values and entirely above the zipf values (max of the lower group < min of the real group);
- that direction matches the prediction that a real text is more uneven along the stream than a shuffled or Zipf stream.

Overlap, or a real mean on the low side, is a fail. C0 is not part of the separation test.

## Predictions
- **V1.** At least one of the four statistics passes.
- **V2.** Scored only if V1 passes. On every passing statistic, each MS hand is closer to the nearer of (mean C0, mean of the three cipher arms) than to the mean of plain Boccaccio. Distance is absolute difference of the means.
- If V1 fails, no tested long-range statistic can see this plaintext, and V2 is not scored.
