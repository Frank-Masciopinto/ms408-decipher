# h45: is the last-glyph link a vowel/consonant pattern? (pre-registration)

Written before any h45 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
The last-glyph to next-first-glyph link is the one MS pattern of natural-language size. How much of it is just a two-way vowel/consonant alternation, in MS, in natural text, and in a generator whose link is a glyph table?

## Measure
Pairs are consecutive words on the same line. The glyph pair is (last unit of word i, first unit of word i+1).

Classes come from `msdecipher.stats.sukhotin`, fit on within-word adjacent units of the same stream. A unit Sukhotin calls a vowel is V. Every other unit that Sukhotin scored is C. Units it did not score are dropped from the pairs.

- **MI_glyph**: mutual information, in bits, of the glyph pair.
- **MI_class**: mutual information of the class pair.
- **share**: MI_class / MI_glyph. A stream with MI_glyph = 0 is excluded.

MS and self-citation and the gap-rule stream use collapsed EVA units. Natural text uses characters.

## Streams
- MS 1A, 2B, 3B: paragraph lines, ZL3b, comma as a word space.
- Controls: Boccaccio, Libro della cucina, Pliny herbal XX–XXVII, Macer Floridus. Lines are the source lines after the same tokenisation the loaders already use. Take the first 10,500 tokens.
- Self-citation: `fitted_s1`, first 10,500 tokens, its own whitespace lines.
- Gap rule: fit `h34_lib.fit_table` on hand 2B lines and apply it with `h34_lib.apply_rule` at strength 1 to the fitted_s1 token stream, seed 45. The table is a glyph rule, not a class rule.

## Predictions
- **C1.** The median share of the four controls is at least 0.25.
- **C2.** Scored only if C1 passes. Every MS hand has a share below the lowest control. That is the pen reading. Otherwise the MS link is not cleanly separated from the controls.
- **C3.** Scored only if C1 passes. Both the self-citation stream and the gap-rule stream have a share below the lowest control.
