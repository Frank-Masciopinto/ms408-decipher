# h51: do diagram/plant/nymph labels match the de-padded seed stems of their folio or quire? (pre-registration)

Written before any h51 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
Earlier tests (`h09` Test 3 and `h17` Test A) checked whether standalone diagram/jar/root/nymph labels (`L` loci) appear verbatim in the *entire* running text of their folio or matched herbal folio, and found only weak or null overlap ($p = 0.09$ within quires for exact matches). However, `h47` and `h48` showed that ~80% of running paragraph text consists of mutated one-edit echoes (`-edy/-ol` swaps, prefix additions) spun out from a smaller set of folio stems. Once we relax matching from exact word identity to **one-edit / stem match** and compare labels specifically against the **de-padded seed stems** (`h49`) and **paragraph-opening headers** (`h50`) of the same folio (and matched herbal–pharma pairs), do the labels anchor the seeds of the paragraph text?

## Data
From `ZL3b-n.txt` via `h09_lib.parse()` and `h09_lib.label_table()`:
- **Labels (`L` loci):** All label words $w$ with $\ge 2$ collapsed EVA units (`len(h13_lib.SEG(w)) >= 2`) on folios that also have paragraph text (`P` loci, at least 20 paragraph tokens), plus the 16 herbal–pharmaceutical same-plant folio pairs from `h09_lib.same_plant_pairs()`.
- **Paragraph representations per folio $f$:**
  1. **`full_para`:** All paragraph tokens on folio $f$.
  2. **`seed_W25` (`h49` de-padded seeds):** Paragraph tokens on folio $f$ remaining after stripping any token within unit edit distance $\le 1$ of an earlier token in the last 25 tokens on folio $f$.
  3. **`header_tokens` (`h50`):** First line (`line == 1`) and paragraph-initial line tokens on folio $f$ (both raw and `d/s/p/t/k/f` onset-stripped).

## Similarity and Hit Measures (between label word $w_L$ on folio $f_L$ and paragraph set $P(f_P)$)
Using collapsed EVA units $u(w) = \text{tuple}(\text{h13\_lib.SEG}(w))$:
1. **`exact_hit`:** $w_L \in P(f_P)$.
2. **`edit1_hit`:** $\exists w \in P(f_P)$ with unit Levenshtein distance $\le 1$ ($\text{lev}(u(w_L), u(w)) \le 1$).
3. **`stem_hit` (onset/suffix-stripped stem match):** After stripping optional leading `o-`/`q-`/`d-`/`s-` (since `h05`/`h09` showed 67% of labels add an `o-` prefix) and comparing the first 3 core units, $\text{stem}_3(w_L) \in \{\text{stem}_3(w) : w \in P(f_P)\}$.

## Three Tests and Nulls (numpy seed 51, 2,000 permutations each)
1. **Test 1 — Own-folio label $\leftrightarrow$ seed enrichment (`own_folio`):**
   - Across all label tokens on folios with paragraph text, compute the fraction of labels with an `exact_hit`, `edit1_hit`, and `stem_hit` in their **own folio's** `seed_W25` set (and `full_para` and `header_tokens`).
   - **Null:** 2,000 permutations of folio assignments **within the same quire** (`h09_lib.perm_within(quire)`), holding each folio's seed set size and each quire's label vocabulary fixed. Also control for folio seed-set size via a degree/length-matched word-sampling null on the same folio.
2. **Test 2 — De-padded herbal $\leftrightarrow$ pharmaceutical same-plant pairs (`same_plant_seeds`):**
   - On the `h09` same-plant pairs `(herbal_folio, pharma_folio)`, compute the fraction of pharmaceutical labels on `pharma_folio` that have an `edit1_hit` or `stem_hit` in the **de-padded `seed_W25`** of the paired `herbal_folio`, scored by mean rank percentile across all herbal folios (and within-hand herbal folios) against 2,000 random partner permutations.
3. **Test 3 — Label-seeded chain length on own folio (`chain_size`):**
   - On each folio with both labels and paragraphs, partition the folio's `seed_W25` stems into **label-linked seeds** (within unit edit distance $\le 1$ or `stem_hit` of a label on that folio) and **unlinked seeds**. Count how many body tokens on that folio lie within unit edit distance $\le 1$ of each seed (**progeny size**). Test whether label-linked seeds generate larger one-edit progeny clusters on their folio than unlinked seeds of the same unit length (2,000 within-folio seed-label permutations).

## Predictions
- **L1 (own-folio label $\leftrightarrow$ seed link):** In Test 1, `edit1_hit` or `stem_hit` between labels and their own folio's `seed_W25` passes the **within-quire** folio permutation null at Holm-adjusted $p < 0.05$ (across the 3 hit types).
- **L2 (de-padded same-plant pairs):** In Test 2, matching pharmaceutical labels to the de-padded `seed_W25` of their paired herbal folio via `edit1_hit` or `stem_hit` achieves a mean rank percentile $> 0.65$ with $p < 0.05$.
- **L3 (label-linked seeds spawn larger paragraph clusters):** In Test 3, label-linked seeds on a folio have significantly larger same-folio one-edit progeny than length-matched unlinked seeds ($p < 0.05$).
