# h43: does vocabulary reset at the page edge? (pre-registration)

Written before any h43 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
h41's word clustering lives inside the folio. Is a folio boundary itself a break in vocabulary, beyond the effect of distance, and do texts with known section edges show the same break?

## Pairs
Paragraph words in reading order. A window is 80 consecutive tokens that all lie on one folio. Two windows form a pair when their start positions differ by 80 tokens or by 160 tokens. A pair is **same** when both windows are on one folio. It is **cross** when a folio boundary falls between them. Similarity is the cosine of the two windows' word-count vectors. The effect at a distance is mean cosine(same) minus mean cosine(cross). A distance is scored only when both sides have at least 30 pairs.

## Null
Inside each quire, rotate the token stream circularly against the quire's own folio lengths, 100 times, numpy seed 43. The folio sizes stay fixed and the text's local order stays fixed. The boundaries move. A distance passes when the observed effect is above the 95th percentile of that null.

## Manuscript
Hands 1A, 2B, 3B, ZL3b, comma as a word space, the h14 loader. Real folios and real quires.

## Controls
Boundaries come from the raw files, before tokenisation. A control is available only if at least 20 boundaries are found. Do not substitute paragraph breaks.

- Boccaccio (`it_boccaccio`): a boundary at each Novella heading.
- Libro della cucina (`it_cucina`): a boundary at each recipe heading in the Zambrini text.
- Pliny, *Naturalis Historia* XX–XXVII (`la_pliny_herbal`): a boundary at each chapter division in the source XML.

Fake folios are the stretches between boundaries. Windows that cross a boundary are invalid, as on a folio. The null rotates the token stream against those stretch lengths.

## Predictions
- **E1.** At distance 80, at least two of the three hands pass the null.
- **E2.** At distance 80, at least two available controls pass the null.
- If fewer than two controls are available, E2 is not scored and the MS result is reported as uncalibrated.
