## Question
h24–h26 miss the fingerprint on word-vs-character dominance (D4), which needs to clear −0.07. Two parts:

1. On the cells already scored, which stored property tracks distance and D4?
2. In a two-knob toy (code length L, suffix-pool size V), does any setting with decodable share ≥ 0.8 also reach D4 ≥ −0.07?

## Part 2 — toy tables
Engine: `h25_lib.NH`, 6 tables, Naibbe card weights, p_unigram 17/36, alphabet size 20 (`h25_lib.GLYPHS`). No copy layer. Plaintext: real Boccaccio. One seed, s = 1, for every cell. Char segmentation (no EVA rendering).

Grid: L ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 5} and V ∈ {8, 16, 32, 64, 128}. 25 cells.

Code strings:

- Unigram and prefix: exactly L glyphs, distinct inside each (state, table). Resample on collision.
- Suffix: one shared pool of V distinct strings of length L, built first. Every suffix slot draws from that pool with replacement. Collisions across letters are intentional.

Scoring: bifolium only (do not score quire). Same D1–D5 definitions as `h20_lib.dstats` and the same MS hand rows and `h20_lib.match`. Cell kind `ctrl`. Decryptability: `h25_lib.decodable_share` on the scored tokens whose label is not None. Workers: 1. Do not edit files outside `hypotheses/h29_d4_toy_tables/`.

## Predictions
- **R1.** On the h26 subset above, `mean_len` has the largest |Spearman ρ| with `m_distance` among the four properties.
- **T1 (main).** No toy cell with decodable share ≥ 0.8 has D4 ≥ −0.07.
- **T2.** Spearman ρ of D4 against V, over the 25 cells, is negative.
