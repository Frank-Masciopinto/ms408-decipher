# h50: do paragraph-initial or line-initial words form a hidden header or acrostic stream? (pre-registration)

Written before any h50 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
`h46` Test 2 showed that the first line of each folio is decoupled from the body's one-edit copy-drift network, and `h14`–`h16` and `h30` showed that paragraph-initial and line-initial positions carry strong onset markers (`d-/s-` and gallows). In medieval steganography (e.g., Trithemius-style word-initial / line-initial concealment), the true message or header list is placed in the first word (or first glyph) of each paragraph or line while the rest of each line is padded out. Do the vertical sequences of **paragraph-initial words**, **line-initial words** (raw or `h16` onset-stripped), or **line-initial first glyphs** in MS 408 exhibit sequential linguistic structure beyond the no-plaintext `h47` generator and permutation nulls?

## Streams and vertical extractions
For MS hands `1A`, `2B`, `3B` (`h30_lib.load_ms(hand)`, `ZL3b-n.txt`, comma as a word space), `h47` synthetic streams (`page_line_copier_1A`, `page_line_copier_2B`, `page_line_copier_3B`), and prose/verse controls (`it_boccaccio`, `dante` from `h30_lib.load_control`), extract four vertical/header sequences per stream in reading order:
1. **`para_words`:** The first word of every paragraph-initial line (`para_first == True`).
2. **`line_words_raw`:** The first word (`pos == 0`) of every paragraph line.
3. **`line_words_stripped`:** The `pos == 0` word of every line after stripping a leading `d`, `s`, `p`, `t`, `k`, `f` onset when the remainder has $\ge 2$ EVA units (`h16` onset stripping).
4. **`line_first_unit`:** The first collapsed EVA unit (`h13_lib.SEG(w)[0]`, or first character for controls) of every line (`pos == 0`), forming a vertical glyph acrostic down each folio.

## Measures
1. **M1 — Vertical word-order gain & repetition avoidance (`D1_vert` and `adj_repeat_z`):**
   - On `line_words_raw`, `line_words_stripped`, and `para_words`, compute 5-fold cross-validated Kneser–Ney bigram gain over unigram ($D_1 = \text{bits}(\text{KN1}) - \text{bits}(\text{KN2})$) and lag-1 class mutual information (top-30 types + OTHER) against 200 within-folio line-order permutations (or within-quire paragraph permutations for `para_words`; seed 50).
2. **M2 — Vertical glyph-acrostic structure (`H2_acrostic_drop` and `sukhotin_alt`):**
   - On `line_first_unit` within each folio, compute conditional bigram entropy $h_2(\text{unit}_\ell \mid \text{unit}_{\ell-1})$ and unigram entropy $H_1(\text{unit}_\ell)$, and define the **acrostic redundancy** $R_{\text{acro}} = H_1 - h_2$ (bits of predictability of line $\ell$'s first glyph from line $\ell-1$'s first glyph on the same folio).
   - Compare $R_{\text{acro}}$ against 200 within-folio line-order permutations (seed 50), and test whether `line_first_unit` shows Sukhotin vowel/consonant alternation excess (as a vertical spelled-out word would) vs horizontal within-word EVA units.
3. **M3 — Folio-header to body seed alignment (`header_body_cos`):**
   - After stripping `d/s/p/t/k/f` onsets from the words on Line 1 of each folio, compute the mean unit-3gram cosine similarity (and one-edit hit rate) between Line 1's stripped words and the rest of the same folio, compared against 200 within-quire permutations of Line-1 headers across folios (seed 50).

## Predictions
- **A1 (vertical word stream has sequential syntax):** In at least two MS hands (`1A`, `2B`, `3B`), either `line_words_stripped` or `para_words` has positive bigram gain ($D_1 > +0.05$ bits/word) **and** lag-1 class MI above the 95th percentile of the 200 permutation draws.
- **A2 (vertical glyph acrostic spells words):** In at least two MS hands, the vertical `line_first_unit` stream has acrostic redundancy $R_{\text{acro}}$ above the 95th percentile of within-folio line permutations **and** reaches at least 50% of the within-word horizontal unit redundancy ($H_1 - h_2$ inside real words of that hand).
- **A3 (onset-stripped Line 1 headers match their own folio):** In at least two MS hands, stripping line-initial onsets from Line 1 yields a same-folio similarity (`header_body_cos`) above the 95th percentile of 200 within-quire Line-1 swaps across folios.
