# h46: how does the folio reset work? (pre-registration)

Written before any h46 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
h43 showed that vocabulary resets at the folio boundary in hands 1A and 2B beyond token distance alone, and h42 showed that one-edit word pairs stay elevated through page-scale lags (`6-20`, `21-100`) and rebound at `101-500` in hand 2B. Three concrete mechanism checks follow from those two results:
1. Do one-edit word pairs themselves drop across a folio boundary at matched token lag (`6-20`, `21-100`, `101-500`)?
2. Do folio-opening words (line 1 of a folio) act as seeds with a higher same-folio one-edit rate than later lines on the same folio?
3. When a pair crosses a single folio boundary in `21-100` or `101-500`, is the one-edit rate higher across a **visible spread** (`verso -> next recto`) than across a **page turn** (`recto -> verso` of the same leaf)?

## Data and units
Paragraph words in reading order from `h14_lib.table()`, ZL3b (`ZL3b-n.txt`), comma as a word space, hands `1A`, `2B`, `3B`. Each word is segmented into collapsed EVA units via `h13_lib.SEG(w)`. Two words form a one-edit pair when `edit1(u_i, u_j)` is true (`Levenshtein.distance(u_i, u_j) == 1` on unit tuples, `u_i != u_j`, `|len(u_i) - len(u_j)| <= 1`). Eligible pairs are pairs with `|len(u_i) - len(u_j)| <= 1` in the specified token-lag bin `j - i in [lo, hi]`.

## Test 1 — Cross-folio drop in one-edit pairs at matched lag
Lag bins: `6-20`, `21-100`, `101-500`. For each hand and lag bin, take all eligible token pairs `(i, j)` in the stream (or up to 20,000 eligible pairs per side via `h42_lib.systematic_ranks` if either side exceeds 20,000).
- **same**: `folio[i] == folio[j]`.
- **cross**: `folio[i] != folio[j]`.
- **Effect**: `rate(same) - rate(cross)`, where `rate` is the fraction of eligible pairs with `edit1 == True`.
- **Null**: 100 circular rotations of the token stream inside each quire against the quire's fixed folio lengths (numpy seed 46, exact `h43` rotation geometry: tokens stay in reading order, folio cut-points rotate). A hand × bin cell is **scored** if both `same` and `cross` have at least 200 eligible pairs, and **passes** if the observed effect exceeds the 95th percentile of the 100 rotation effects.

## Test 2 — Folio-opening line seed test
On each folio with at least 6 paragraph lines:
- **line-1 seed rate**: fraction of eligible pairs `(i, j)` on the **same folio** (`i < j`, `|len(u_i) - len(u_j)| <= 1`) where token `i` is on the first paragraph line of that folio and token `j` is on any later line of that folio that have `edit1(u_i, u_j) == True`.
- **interior baseline rate**: fraction of eligible pairs `(i, j)` on the **same folio** (`i < j`, on different lines of that folio) where neither token is on the first paragraph line of the folio that have `edit1(u_i, u_j) == True`.
- **Effect**: `seed_rate - interior_rate`, pooled across folios of a hand.
- **Null**: 200 permutations of line order within each folio (preserving line lengths and token multisets per line; numpy seed 46). Passes if observed effect exceeds the 95th percentile of the null.

## Test 3 — Visible spread (`v -> r`) vs page turn (`r -> v`)
For cross-folio eligible pairs `(i, j)` in lag bins `21-100` and `101-500` where `folio[i]` and `folio[j]` are consecutive folios in the manuscript (`folio_index[j] == folio_index[i] + 1`):
- **page_turn (`r_to_v`)**: `folio[i]` ends with `'r'` (recto) and `folio[j]` ends with `'v'` (verso).
- **open_spread (`v_to_r`)**: `folio[i]` ends with `'v'` (verso) and `folio[j]` ends with `'r'` (recto).
- **Effect**: `rate(v_to_r) - rate(r_to_v)`.
- **Null**: 200 random swaps of the `r_to_v` / `v_to_r` labels at the folio-transition level within each hand (numpy seed 46). Scored if both transition types have at least 200 eligible pairs; passes if observed effect exceeds the 95th percentile of the null.

## Predictions
- **F1 (folio boundary breaks one-edit chains):** In Test 1, at least one scored lag bin (`21-100` or `101-500`) passes the rotation null in both Hand 1A and Hand 2B.
- **F2 (first-line seeding):** In Test 2, at least two of the three hands (`1A`, `2B`, `3B`) pass the line-order permutation null (`seed_rate > interior_rate`).
- **F3 (open-spread copying in Hand 2B):** In Test 3, Hand 2B passes the transition-label null (`rate(v_to_r) > rate(r_to_v)`) in `21-100` or `101-500`.
