# h42: where do one-edit near-repeats die out? (pre-registration)

Written before any h42 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
h39's tuned copier matched MS's local near-repeat rate and overshot the 5-word copy rate. Is MS's excess of one-edit word pairs short-ranged, page-ranged, or flat, and does that copier stay elevated further out than MS does?

## Pairs
Unit edit distance 1. MS, C0, self-citation and h39 streams use collapsed EVA units. Boccaccio uses characters. Compare two words only when their unit lengths differ by at most 1.

Distance is the number of tokens between the two words along the stream. Bins: `1`, `2-5`, `6-20`, `21-100`, `101-500`, `501-1000`.

For each bin, take up to 4,000 pairs by a fixed stride over the eligible pairs. The same count and stride are used for the null.

## Null
Shuffle tokens inside each hand × quire, preserving the multiset and the line/page lengths. Controls and generated streams have no quires: shuffle inside blocks of 8 fake pages of 200 tokens. Ten shuffles, numpy seed 42. Excess = observed rate minus the mean null rate. A bin is elevated when excess > 2 × the shuffle standard deviation and the null sd is above 0.

## Streams
- MS 1A, 2B, 3B: paragraph lines, ZL3b, comma as a space, the whole hand.
- Boccaccio: first 10,500 tokens, fake pages of 200.
- C0: one h12 C0 stream of 10,500 from hand 2B, generation seed 1. Fit seed 0.
- Self-citation: `data/mechanisms/selfcitation/fitted_s1.txt`, whitespace tokens, fake pages of 200, first 10,500 tokens.
- h39: `lines_2B_native_tuned.json` and `lines_2B_C0_graph.json` under `hypotheses/h39_graph_respell_and_tuned_copier/results/`.

## Shape
The decay point is the first bin, in the order above, that is not elevated, after at least one earlier bin was elevated. If no bin is elevated, the shape is `none`. If every bin is elevated, the shape is `flat`.

- `local`: decay point is `2-5` or `6-20`.
- `page`: decay point is `21-100` or `101-500`.
- `long`: decay point is `501-1000`.
- `flat`: no decay point.

## Predictions
- **N1.** MS 2B is `local` or `page`, not `flat` or `long`.
- **N2.** The h39 native tuned stream's decay point is later than MS 2B's, or it is `flat` while MS 2B is not.
- **N3.** Boccaccio's excess in bin `1` is smaller than MS 2B's excess in bin `1`.
