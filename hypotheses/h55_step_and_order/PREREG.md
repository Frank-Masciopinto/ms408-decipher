# h55: neighbouring paragraph steps, and word order inside the line (pre-registration)

Written before any h55 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

h52, h53 and h54 are already computed. Their numbers are not inputs to these pass rules. h54's within-line core shuffle and its middle-line contrast are not repeated.

## Question
Two residuals are unresolved.

- The second unit of a word predicts the second unit of the next word after the touching junction is held fixed (h52). Removing near-copies does not remove it (h54 A1). The shuffle used in h54 A2 also pulled second units off their own words, and the copier failed it too, so it did not separate adjacency from a shared inventory.
- Opening and closing interiors of a paragraph differ (h53). In h54 the lines used as the comparison sat about two lines closer together than the opening and closing lines, and the closing lines were shorter, so drift and length were still in the contrast.

Does a null that keeps every word intact still find adjacent order? Are the first step and the last step of a paragraph different from every other single step, once every comparison is exactly one line apart and the edge words are gone?

## Streams
Hands `1A`, `2B`, `3B`, and `page_line_copier` for those hands, on the `h47` template (`h47_lib.load_template`, up to 10,500 tokens). Copier words come from `generated_lines` in `hypotheses/h47_page_line_generator/results/streams.json`, zipped onto that template. Line counts and line lengths must match.

Slots and F2 are exactly as in h52. Units are collapsed EVA units. `core` is the second unit, or `SHORT` when the word has fewer than three units. A core, onset, or end label below 30 tokens becomes `OTHER`. `SHORT` is never rebinned. F2 is `I(core_t ; core_{t+1} | end_t, onset_{t+1})` for successive words on the same line. Strata with fewer than 20 pairs are left out of the sum.

A paragraph is a maximal run of lines, split at `para_first` and at a folio change.

## Plants and the quiet check
Both tests are run on the copier and on a planted copier before the manuscript result is interpreted.

- **Order plant.** Inside each copier line, stably sort the words by `core`. Words are otherwise unchanged.
- **Step plant.** On the copier, if a line has at least 7 words, replace the words strictly inside positions 2 through n−3 (that is, `words[2:-2]`) with `PLANT` on the first line of each paragraph and with `ENDPLANT` on the last line. Line lengths stay the same.

A copier hand is **quiet** when it does not meet the pass rule for that test. A test is **valid** only when the copier is quiet on at least two hands and the matching plant meets the plant rule on at least two hands. Otherwise that test is `invalid` and its manuscript passes are not interpreted.

The plant rule for the order tests is `excess >= 0.02` and `z >= 2.58`. The plant rule for a step test is: the planted value is strictly above its null 95th percentile and `excess >= 0.05`.

## Part S — order inside the line

### S1, whole-word shuffle
The null permutes the order of whole words inside each line. A line with fewer than 2 words is unchanged. Onset, core and end of a word stay together. 200 permutations, numpy seed 55, its own generator. `excess = observed − mean(null)`. `z = excess / sd(null)`, with `sd` the population standard deviation. If `sd < 1e-12`, `z = 0`.

A hand passes S1 when at least 500 pairs enter F2, `excess >= 0.01`, `z >= 2.58`, and `excess(MS) − excess(copier) >= 0.01`.

### S2, what the previous core adds beyond the folio
For each line, the training pairs are the successive pairs on the other lines of the same folio. With add-0.5 smoothing, on that training set, compare

- `p0 = P(core_{t+1} | end_t, onset_{t+1})`
- `p1 = P(core_{t+1} | end_t, onset_{t+1}, core_t)`

The core alphabet is the training alphabet plus `OTHER`. A test pair whose junction never occurs in training is skipped. An unseen core is scored as `OTHER`. The gain is the mean of `log2 p1 − log2 p0` over the pairs that remain.

The null is the same whole-word shuffle, 200 times, seed 55, a new generator. A hand passes S2 when at least 500 pairs were scored, `excess >= 0.01`, `z >= 2.58`, and the excess exceeds the copier's excess by `>= 0.01`.

### What S says
If S1 is invalid, the order result is `invalid`. Otherwise:

- `adjacency` if S1 passes on at least two hands
- `no_adjacency` if S1 passes on no hand
- `partial` if S1 passes on one hand

S2 gets the same three labels, named `bits`, `no_bits` and `partial_bits`, or `invalid` when S2 itself is invalid. S2 does not change the S1 label.

## Part P — one-line steps

An interior is `words[2:-2]`, and only on a line of at least 7 words. A neighbouring pair is eligible when both lines have at least 7 words. A paragraph enters the opening test when it has at least two eligible pairs and the pair `(line 0, line 1)` is eligible. It enters the closing test when it has at least two eligible pairs and the pair `(line n−2, line n−1)` is eligible.

Word classes are the 40 most frequent interiors in all eligible pairs of the paragraphs that enter that test, frozen, plus `OTHER`. The statistic is the Jensen–Shannon divergence, log base 2, between the interiors of the left lines and the interiors of the right lines of the designated pairs.

The null, 200 times, seed 55, its own generator for the opening test and another for the closing test: inside each paragraph, choose one eligible neighbouring pair uniformly and treat it as the designated pair. The 95th percentile of 200 finite draws is the sorted value at index 189. A hand is unscored unless each side has at least 200 tokens and all 200 draws are finite. Unscored hands do not pass.

A hand passes when it is scored, the observed divergence is strictly above the null 95th percentile, `excess >= 0.01`, and the excess exceeds the copier's excess by `>= 0.01`.

### What P says
Opening and closing are judged separately. A side is `invalid` when its validity check fails. If both sides are invalid, the paragraph result is `invalid`. Otherwise:

- `role` if at least one valid side passes on at least two hands
- `no_role` if every valid side passes on no hand
- `partial` otherwise

## Part X — shared onset shift
This is computed only when the paragraph result is `role`. The step used is the opening step if that side passed on at least two hands, and otherwise the closing step.

For each interior word, take its first EVA unit. Keep a unit only when it has at least 20 tokens in the left bag plus the right bag. For each kept unit,

`log ratio = log((n_right + 0.5) / N_right) − log((n_left + 0.5) / N_left)`.

For each pair of hands, align the units kept in both. If fewer than 8 units remain, the pair is unscored. Otherwise compute the Spearman correlation.

The null redraws the designated pairs with the Part P rule, 200 times, seed 55, a new generator, and recomputes the three correlations. A pair passes when it is scored, the correlation is `>= 0.30`, and it is strictly above the null 95th percentile (index 189 of 200 finite draws).

- `shared` if at least two hand pairs pass
- `not_shared` otherwise

If the paragraph result is not `role`, X is `not_run`.
