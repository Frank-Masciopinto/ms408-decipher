# h44: can a generator without plaintext match MS page clustering? (pre-registration)

Written before any h44 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
MS word clustering is concentrated inside the folio. Can fitted self-citation, the C0 copy-and-modify engine, an h06 section pool, or the h39 copiers reach that excess without being handed a new vocabulary on every page?

## Statistic
`h41_lib.burst`: among the 50 most frequent types, the mean of (count in the busiest block of 200 tokens) / (count / number of blocks).

For a stream with page ids and quire ids, compute four scores, each the mean of 10 shuffles (numpy seed 44) except the original:

- **orig**: the stream as generated.
- **in_page**: tokens shuffled inside each page.
- **in_quire**: tokens shuffled inside each quire.
- **full**: tokens shuffled across the whole stream.

Excess = orig − full. The share kept inside the quire is (in_quire − full) / excess when excess > 0.

## Pages
For generated streams, a page is L consecutive tokens and a quire is 8 pages. L is the median number of paragraph tokens on a folio of hand 2B, computed and recorded. Streams are 10,500 tokens.

MS hands 1A, 2B and 3B use their real folios and real quires, first 10,500 paragraph tokens (1A may be shorter). They are the reference, recomputed with this code.

## Streams
- `fitted_s1` and `fitted_s2` from `data/mechanisms/selfcitation/`.
- C0: one stream, `h12` fit on hand 2B paragraph words (seed 0, `n_random` 20) and generation seed 1.
- h06 section pool: `h06_lib.sv_generate` with the default base parameters, no parameter search. One stream has a pool of the 10 most frequent hand-2B words and `p_pool` 0.5, constant across the stream. One stream has `p_pool` 0. Record that the parameters were not fitted.
- h39: `lines_2B_native_tuned.json` and `lines_2B_C0_graph.json`, concatenated in order.
- Positive control: each page is drawn from its own vocabulary of 40 types that no other page uses, Zipf frequencies inside the page. This stream is handed a new vocabulary every page.

## Predictions
- **B1.** Every stream except the positive control and the three MS hands has excess below the smallest MS hand excess.
- **B2.** The positive control has excess above the largest MS hand excess.
- **B3.** For at least two MS hands, in_page − full is greater than in_quire − full.
