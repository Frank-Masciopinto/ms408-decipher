# h53: do paragraph interiors change from the opening line to the closing line? (pre-registration)

Written before any h53 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
`h30` showed that paragraph-opening lines start with a gallows word and that line-final words prefer `-am`/`-dy`, and `h47` builds both habits in as line-edge weights. After those edge words are removed, a discourse or recipe language can still use different vocabulary in the **middle of the first line** and the **middle of the last line** of a paragraph. The page-and-line copier cannot: once the first and last words of a line are ignored, it has no paragraph role. Does MS 408?

## Streams
- **MS and copier,** same span: `h47_lib.load_template(hand)` for hands `1A`, `2B`, `3B` (up to 10,500 tokens). Copier words are `generated_lines` for `page_line_copier_{hand}` in `hypotheses/h47_page_line_generator/results/streams.json`, zipped onto that template. Line counts and line lengths must match.
- **Recipe control:** `it_cucina`, `h30_lib.load_control("italian/pg33954.txt")` (10,000 tokens). This control is the sensitivity check C1. It is not a hand in A1.

## Paragraphs and roles
A paragraph is a maximal run of lines.

- On MS and the copier, a new paragraph starts when `para_first` is true or the folio id changes.
- On `it_cucina`, a new paragraph starts only when `para_first` is true. Its `page` field is a token block, not a folio, and is not a boundary.

Keep paragraphs with at least 3 lines. Line 0 is `OPEN`, the last line is `CLOSE`, and the lines between them are `MID`.

## Tokens that count
From every line, drop the first word and the last word. A line with fewer than 4 words contributes nothing. The words that remain are the line interior. This removes the paragraph-initial gallows word and the line-initial and line-final habits already modelled by `h47`.

A stream is **scored** only if the `OPEN` interiors and the `CLOSE` interiors each contain at least 200 words.

## Statistic
Freeze a word-class map on the original interiors of that stream: the 40 most frequent interior words, and `OTHER` for the rest.

`S` is the Jensen–Shannon divergence, log base 2, between the class counts of `OPEN` interiors and the class counts of `CLOSE` interiors (`h30_lib.js`).

Null: 200 permutations, numpy seed 53. Inside each kept paragraph, shuffle the order of its lines, then recompute `OPEN` and `CLOSE` interiors and `S` with the **same** class map.

- `excess = observed − mean(null)`
- The 95th percentile is the 190th order statistic of the 200 null values (index 189 in 0-based sorted order).
- `z = excess / sd(null)`. If `sd(null) < 1e-12`, `z = 0`.

Onset-class and end-class versions of the same contrast (unit 0 and last unit of each interior word; labels below 30 tokens become `OTHER`) are computed with the same null and reported. They do not enter A1 or C1.

## Predictions
A scored hand passes when all of these hold:

- observed `S` is strictly greater than the null 95th percentile
- `excess >= 0.01` bits
- `excess(MS) − excess(page_line_copier of the same hand) >= 0.01` bits

- **A1:** at least two of the three hands are scored and pass.
- **C1:** `it_cucina` is scored, its observed `S` is strictly greater than its null 95th percentile, and its `excess >= 0.01`.

## How to read the pair of outcomes
- A1 true: paragraph role survives after line edges are removed, and it is larger than in the copier.
- A1 false and C1 true: this contrast detects a real recipe arc, and the hands do not show one beyond the copier.
- A1 false and C1 false: the contrast did not detect the recipe control, so a failed A1 is inconclusive.
- Unscored streams do not pass.
