# h47: unified page- and line-bounded generator (pre-registration)

Written before any h47 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question
Earlier stages solved individual properties in isolation: `h34` added the cross-word glyph junction rule, `h39` preserved the one-edit network and hit the multi-transcriber space band (`h40`), `h44` matched page-level burstiness, `h43` showed a vocabulary break at physical folio edges, and `h30` showed that lines are closed units with strong line-initial and line-final markers and weak cross-line dependency. Can a **single no-plaintext generator** that combines (a) folio-edge memory resets, (b) line-boundary framing, and (c) local self-citation pass all of these benchmarks on the same generated stream?

## Data and split
Hands `1A`, `2B`, `3B`, paragraph words and lines from `h14_lib.table()` / `h30_lib.load_ms(hand)`, ZL3b (`ZL3b-n.txt`), comma as a word space.
- For train/test generator fitting: even bifolia = train, odd bifolia = test (`run_h34.split(hand)`), matching `h34`–`h39`.
- Real folio and line lengths of the target hand (`h30_lib.load_ms(hand)`) provide the layout template (quire, folio, line lengths, and `para_first` flags) up to `N = 10,500` tokens.

## Generator architecture (`page_line_copier`)
Fit on train bifolia of each hand (`1A`, `2B`, `3B`), generate on the layout template (up to 10,500 tokens, seed 47):
1. **Base word and mutation model:** `h35` character/unit shape model `m` fit on train interior words, plus `h39` tuned native copier parameters `(p_fresh, window, p0)` for that hand.
2. **Folio-edge reset rule (`h43`):** Each folio maintains its own active lookback buffer of generated stems. At the start of a new folio, only a fraction `rho_keep = 0.15` of the previous folio's lookback buffer is retained; the rest of the buffer is cleared so new fresh stems drawn on that folio dominate local one-edit copying on that page.
3. **Line-boundary framing (`h30`):**
   - **Line-start / paragraph-start:** On the first word of a line (`pos == 0`), the cross-word junction rule is **not** applied across the line break (resetting the cross-line link). Instead, `pos == 0` candidates are weighted by empirical train `P(onset | para_first)` on paragraph-initial lines (`para_first == True`) and `P(onset | line_initial)` + top line-initial types on other lines.
   - **Line-interior (`0 < pos < L - 1`):** Candidates are drawn from the folio's active lookback buffer (with one-edit mutation) or fresh shape model, weighted by the `h34` cross-word junction table `r(first | prev_last)^s*`.
   - **Line-end (`pos == L - 1`):** Candidates are weighted by both `r(first | prev_last)^s*` and empirical train `P(end_unit | line_final)` / line-final ending weights (`-am/-dam/-dy` / final `-y/-m/-l`).

Comparison baselines on Hand 2B: stored `h39` native tuned (`lines_2B_native_tuned.json`) and `h39` C0 graph (`lines_2B_C0_graph.json`), which have uniform 9-word lines and no folio resets or line-edge rules.

## Benchmarks (scored per hand on the generated stream)
1. **B1 — Likelihood fingerprint (`h20`):** Bifolium D1–D5 match count (`h20_lib.dstats` and `h20_lib.match`, target $\ge 4/5$).
2. **B2 — Widened space band & junction (`h40`):** Interior space predictability $S$ inside the hand's `h40` multi-transcriber band ±0.02 (`1A`: `[0.730, 0.790]`, `2B`: `[0.791, 0.847]`, `3B`: `[0.795, 0.867]`), and within-line junction $J \ge 0.06$ bits.
3. **B3 — Line-edge structure (`h30`):**
   - Positional excess MI (`M2` for `ONSET` + `END`) $\ge 3\times$ the `h30` hard-wrapped prose control mean, with $z > 5$;
   - Cross-line `END -> ONSET` MI excess $< 50\%$ of within-line `END -> ONSET` MI (`L3`);
   - Paragraph-initial onset JS $z > 3$ (`L4`).
4. **B4 — Folio-boundary reset (`h43`):** Passes the `h43` distance-80 rotation null (observed `same - cross` cosine effect $>$ 95th percentile of 100 within-quire rotations, numpy seed 43).
5. **B5 — Page burstiness (`h44`):** 200-token burstiness excess (overall shuffle minus observed) in `[0.8, 3.5]` and folio-concentrated (`shuf_overall - shuf_folio > shuf_quire - shuf_folio`).
6. **B6 — Page-scale one-edit decay (`h42`):** Elevated in `1`, `2-5`, `6-20`, `21-100` and decays (`not elevated`) by `101-500` or `501-1000` (`shape == "page"`).

## Predictions
- **U1 (fingerprint + space + junction):** At least 2 of the 3 hands pass both **B1** ($\ge 4/5$ fingerprint) and **B2** (space band and $J \ge 0.06$).
- **U2 (line unit + folio break):** At least 2 of the 3 hands pass both **B3** (all three line-structure criteria) and **B4** (`h43` distance-80 folio-edge break, among hands with $\ge 30$ cross pairs).
- **U3 (page burst + page decay):** At least 2 of the 3 hands pass both **B5** (folio-concentrated burstiness) and **B6** (`shape == "page"` in `h42`), and on Hand 2B `page_line_copier` beats both `h39` baselines on total passed benchmarks (out of B1–B6).
