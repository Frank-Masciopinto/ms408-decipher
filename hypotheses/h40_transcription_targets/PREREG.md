# h40: do the h33–h39 targets survive a change of transcription? (pre-registration)

Written before any h40 computing. No translation claims. Do not edit this file after it is committed.

An exploratory check, already run and therefore not a prediction, recomputed h33's space predictability and last-glyph link on the same loader. Interior space predictability moved by 0.017–0.030 per hand across the conditions below, and the link dropped by 15–32% when uncertain spaces were joined. Those numbers are a baseline. This stage adds the likelihood fingerprint, which was not computed, and re-scores stored generator cells against a band widened by the transcriptions.

## Question
The h33–h39 loop scored one transcription (ZL3b) with uncertain spaces counted as word spaces. Do space predictability, the last-to-first glyph link, and the h20 fingerprint D1–D5 stay inside the tolerances used there when the transcription or the space rule changes? After widening each hand's space band to the observed range, how many stored h39 cells fall inside it?

## Conditions
Hands 1A, 2B and 3B. Paragraph lines only, the h30/h14 loader. Five readings of the same pages:

- `zl_split`: `data/transcriptions/ZL3b-n.txt`, comma is a word space (the current loader).
- `zl_join`: same file, comma deleted before splitting, so the two sides of an uncertain space become one word.
- `it2a`: `IT2a-n.txt`, which marks no uncertain spaces.
- `rf_split`: `RF1b-e.txt`, comma is a word space.
- `rf_join`: `RF1b-e.txt`, comma deleted.

GC2a is excluded: its alphabet is not EVA. Do not modify the shared loader files. Point the existing loader at a transcription path for the duration of a condition, and restore it afterwards.

## Measures
- Space predictability and junction excess: `h33_lib.space_predict` and `h33_lib.junction` (numpy seed 33), on the first 10,326 tokens per hand (the h33 cap), full lines and line-interior lines. This repeats the exploratory check; report it, do not treat agreement with it as a new result.
- Fingerprint: D1–D5 from `h20_lib.dstats` and `h20_lib.match`, bifolium folds only, cell kind `ctrl`, EVA segmentation, up to 10,500 tokens. One cell per hand × condition (the text is fixed). Skip the quire scheme. Delete `tokens_*.csv.gz` after each cell.
- Stored cells: the nine h39 variant-A (graph) rows in `hypotheses/h39_graph_respell_and_tuned_copier/README.md`. Do not rerun those generators.

## Bands
- Old space band, as used in h35–h39: interior predictability in [0.72, 0.80], same for every hand.
- Widened space band, per hand: the min and max of interior predictability across the five conditions.
- Fingerprint tolerance: `h20_lib.DELTA` (D1 0.10, D2 0.10, D3 0.015, D4 0.10, D5 0.15).

## Predictions
- **R1.** For every hand and every one of D1–D5, the range across the five conditions is at most that statistic's DELTA. Otherwise the fingerprint target itself moves with the transcription.
- **R2.** At least one of the nine h39-A cells has a space predictability outside [0.72, 0.80] and inside its hand's widened band.
- **R3.** Junction excess stays above 0.05 in all 15 hand × condition cells, and above the stored generator values in `hypotheses/h33_word_boundary_resegmentation/results/` (all ≤ 0.004). A condition at or below 0.05 means the link does not survive that reading.
